# Reading the answers

The paste starts with a marker and lists every item by id:

```
<!-- html-interactive v1 · page=offsite-2026 · answered=3/4 · 2026-03-02T14:10Z -->
## Place and dates
- `city` Which city hosts it? → **Lyon** (recommended was: Porto)
  - note: cheaper trains for the Paris group
- `week` Which week? → **Second week of March** (recommended, accepted in bulk)
- `partners` Partners at the dinner → PENDING
```

1. Check `page=` is the page you are waiting on; an older round can carry the same item ids.
2. Map answers by the id in backticks, never by title or position.

| You see | Do |
|---|---|
| `PENDING` / `PENDIENTE` | Treat as open; never fill it with your recommendation |
| `(recommended)` | Act on it |
| `(recommended, accepted in bulk)` | Act on it; name anything irreversible in your summary first |
| `(recommended was: X)` | Follow the reader's choice; the note says why |
| `(note only)` | The note is the answer |
| "Orphaned answers" | Answers to an earlier version: ask whether they still hold |

Lines starting with `>` are free notes: read them as text, not as answers. Before acting, reply with what you will do and what stays open.
